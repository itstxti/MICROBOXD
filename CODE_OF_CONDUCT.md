# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be kind and respectful. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination and spam are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or discussions. Assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and welcome newcomers.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this and may ban repeat offenders. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile ([@itstxti](https://github.com/itstxti)).
